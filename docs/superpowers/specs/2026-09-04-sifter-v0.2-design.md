# SIFTER v0.2 design

## Product statement

SIFTER v0.2 fits one spectrum quickly and transparently, supports at least ten peaks,
shows the observations in real and Fourier space before fitting, and can optionally use a
trusted fit to initialize related spectra without forcing the new result.

Version 0.2 preserves the central scientific contract from v0.1: final parameters,
information criteria, diagnostics, and uncertainty are computed from the original-domain
observations. Smoothing, Fourier analysis, windowed fitting, and previous results may
propose or initialize models but may not replace the measured spectrum.

## Release structure

The release is divided into independently testable workstreams:

1. input reliability and interpretable results;
2. bounded staged search and runtime instrumentation;
3. deterministic parallel execution and live progress;
4. pre-fit real-space and Fourier-space previews;
5. windowed progressive initialization with global refinement;
6. measurement context and optional reference-assisted fitting;
7. related-spectrum visualization, validation, and release packaging.

Related-spectrum tracking is the first workstream that may move to v0.2.1 if it threatens
the single-spectrum performance and transparency goals.

## Input reliability

The table loader retains automatic comma, tab, semicolon, and whitespace inference while
adding explicit controls for delimiter, header presence, and leading metadata rows. It must
support ordinary two-column tab-separated text files, including headers with units. Parsing
failures report structural facts and source line numbers without copying observation values
into logs.

The GUI exposes the inferred delimiter and header, allows correction before analysis, and
shows a structural preview. It never silently repairs or discards malformed numeric rows.

## Interpretable results

The GUI displays the fitted peak table, 95% uncertainty intervals when available, residual
diagnostics, measurement units, Fourier applicability, and definitions for BIC, delta BIC,
AICc, RMSE, baseline order, and residual sign. It states that information criteria compare
candidates on the same observations and are not probabilities.

Interactive and exported figures use the same component convention. Peak area is clearly
identified as integrated area rather than height. Expanded result records use
`sifter.fit_result.v2`; v0.1 behavior remains documented and compatibility-tested.

## Staged search and execution

The default maximum peak count is at least ten. Standard mode does not exhaustively cross
all counts, baselines, and shapes. It preprocesses once, constructs a detector-informed
peak-count window, screens candidates cheaply, retains diverse candidates, refines them in
parallel, globally fits finalists, ranks them by BIC with AICc visible, and then estimates
uncertainty.

Every nonlinear optimizer call has an explicit evaluation budget. Coarse budget-limited
fits are provisional and can never be exported as final results. Screening solutions warm
start refinement and final fitting. Exhaustive mode remains available for confirmation.

Candidate models, not starts within one model, are parallelized with spawn-safe process
workers. Numerical thread pools are limited inside workers. Seeds are generated in the
parent from stable candidate indices so serial and parallel runs are equivalent within
declared numerical tolerances.

Progress events cover preprocessing, screening, expansion, refinement, final fitting,
uncertainty, and completion. Workers do not call Streamlit.

## Windowed progressive initialization

Windowing is an initialization and search-reduction mechanism, not a definition of the
final fit. Significant maxima are grouped using baseline-adjusted prominence, width,
derivative evidence, noise scale, and intervening valleys. Each group receives a core and
an overlapping halo. Gaussian-only halos begin at three local FWHM; searches admitting
Lorentzian or Voigt components begin at six local FWHM and never fewer than ten coordinate
steps. Excessively overlapping windows merge automatically.

Windows are fitted in parallel. A window sees its halo but owns only peaks centered in its
core, preventing duplicate boundary peaks. Strong isolated regions may be fitted first;
residuals from their provisional sum may propose additional regions in a later parallel
wave. Such subtraction is used only for proposal generation.

Local baselines are nuisance terms and are never summed. Local information criteria rank
models only within a window and are never added across overlapping windows. A bounded beam
of assembled candidates is jointly refined against the complete, untouched spectrum with
one global baseline. Only these global fits receive final BIC, AICc, diagnostics, and
uncertainty.

If decomposition is unsafe, all proposed windows merge or Standard mode falls back to a
global search. Exhaustive mode remains global.

## One resolved maximum per component

By default, a fitted component must not explain more than one statistically resolved
maximum. A maximum is resolved only when it is prominent relative to noise, stable across
multiple smoothing scales, supported by derivative or curvature evidence, and separated
by a meaningful valley.

Each resolved maximum defines a peak basin. Initial width bounds derive from neighboring
valleys. After local and global fitting, SIFTER computes every component's effective FWHM
and counts resolved maxima within it. A violating fit is retried with tightened bounds or
split initialization. Persistent violations make that candidate structurally inadmissible
for final ranking. If resolution evidence is ambiguous, SIFTER warns rather than forcing a
split.

An advanced explicit override may admit a known physical broad band underneath several
sharper features. The override and its consequences are recorded in the result. It is off
by default.

## Measurement context and related spectra

Temperature, laser power, and generic conditions are provenance in v0.2. Temperature is
stored in kelvin and must be nonnegative. Laser power accepts nW, uW, microW, mW, and W,
canonicalizes to watts, and preserves the user's display value. No hidden heating,
normalization, or linewidth physics is applied.

Given identical observations, uncertainties, seed, and fit configuration, adding context
must not change a single-spectrum fit.

A `FitReference` may seed candidates for a related spectrum. Ordinary detection, Fourier
analysis, candidate generation, adaptive expansion, and independent global BIC ranking
still run. A deliberately wrong reference must not force the new result.

Independently fitted session results may be plotted by temperature or laser power. Peak
tracking uses conservative center-and-width assignment and reports ambiguous appearance,
disappearance, merging, or crossing instead of forcing identity. No global temperature- or
power-dependent physical model is fitted in v0.2.

## Validation and release gates

Runtime benchmarks record candidate counts, optimizer calls, function evaluations, wall
time, selected model, and recovery errors. The three-peak performance comparison and
well-resolved and partially overlapping ten-peak cases are mandatory. A threefold speedup
on a documented four-core benchmark is a release target, not a flaky wall-clock CI test.

CI enforces deterministic serial/parallel equivalence, bounded work, staged-versus-
exhaustive quality comparisons, Windows spawn safety, scientific recovery, Ruff, strict
mypy, at least 90% coverage, and successful package builds on supported Python versions.
Private inputs and generated analyses remain excluded from version control.

## Explicit deferrals

Version 0.2 does not add global simultaneous fitting over temperature or laser power,
Varshni or linewidth physics, heating or saturation models, Bayesian trans-dimensional
sampling, neural peak inference, GPU or distributed execution, two-dimensional spectra,
or cloud processing.
